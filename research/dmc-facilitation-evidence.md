Actual English DMC video-script text is in `/home/yeshu/projects/leviathan/eng/course/txts/`, not a Git repository. The directory contains scripts `eng.eng.03.009.001.1080.txt` through `eng.eng.03.009.010.1080.txt`. The strongest facilitation evidence follows; line numbers refer to those original plain-text files.

## Direct script guidance

- **Facilitate discovery, not answers.** Script 003, lines 59 and 69: “No matter what, we don’t answer their questions. We always ask, ‘What does the Bible say?’” Lines 49–55 describe group correction rather than dependence on a knowledgeable expert.[1]
- **Let the group retell and correct against the passage.** Script 003, line 51: “Now, let’s have someone retell this passage in their own words, as though they were telling a friend who has never heard it. Let's help them if they leave anything out or add anything by mistake. If that happens we can ask, ‘Where do you find that in the story?’”[1]
- **Ask, pause, let people take turns.** Script 003, lines 33–41 models one question, group turns responding, then the next question; the facilitator “did was press play and pause.” Lines 37–39 apply the same hear-then-discuss pattern to the Bible story.[1]
- **Keep the three-part DBS structure.** Script 008, line 41: fellowship/accountability → hearing or reading Scripture → discussion and application commitments. Lines 45–47 say no expert or prepared sermon is needed: ask the questions and keep the group on track.[3]
- **Elicit concrete commitments and revisit them.** Script 004, line 45 asks “What is a specific action or thing you will do?” and next time asks “In our last meeting, you decided to apply what you learned. What did you do, and how did it go?”[2]
- **Include sharing.** Script 009, line 97: “Who will you share a truth from this story with before we meet again? Do you know others who would also like to discover God's word in this app like we are?”[4]
- **Default lesson progression is Creation through Foundations.** Script 008, line 57 permits a few Topics stories for felt needs or an unready group, but states the goal is starting with creation and working through the foundational sets.[3]
- **Avoid creating facilitator dependence.** Script 008, lines 59–63 teaches model, equip, watch, leave, with continuing coaching rather than permanent expert leadership.[3]

## Prototype implications (design interpretation, not script quotations)

Use the canonical lesson questions in sequence. Speak briefly, then listen; do not insert a sermon, authoritative interpretation, or the agent's own answer. Redirect theological questions to the passage and invite participants to respond. Invite a human retelling, allow the group to identify additions/omissions, and replay the passage when helpful. Track whose turn it is and their own commitments, without inventing commitments or declaring interpretations correct. Commands such as repeat, pause, and next are facilitation controls, not theological questions, and can be answered normally.

Do not confuse the three-part ordinary DBS with the four-part DMC training meeting (003, line 225), which adds a training video. The reviewed scripts do not specify ASR silence timeouts, interruption rules, or exact round-robin enforcement; those remain product decisions. The quoted no-answer rule should not be used to withhold immediate safety assistance in abuse or crisis disclosures.
